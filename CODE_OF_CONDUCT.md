# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors are expected to maintain a respectful, inclusive and
evidence-oriented environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss methods and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Report uncertainty, limitations and negative results honestly.
- Respect dataset licenses, privacy and access restrictions.
- Avoid overstating experimental claims.
- Give constructive, specific review feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate publication of
restricted data, credential exposure, or falsification of experimental
evidence is not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through an appropriate private
GitHub channel. The maintainer may edit, reject or remove contributions and
restrict participation when necessary.
